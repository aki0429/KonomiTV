Go source availability
The exact unmodified module paths/versions are listed in go-modules.txt, and
their license/notice files are in go/. Source can be obtained through the Go
module proxy (https://proxy.golang.org) or the projects' source repositories.
For modernc.org/sqlite and its modernc.org dependencies, the corresponding
module source archives are available at:
https://proxy.golang.org/modernc.org/sqlite/@v/v1.60.1.zip
https://proxy.golang.org/modernc.org/libc/@v/v1.77.1.zip
https://proxy.golang.org/modernc.org/memory/@v/v1.12.1.zip
https://proxy.golang.org/modernc.org/mathutil/@v/v1.7.1.zip
No changes are made to those module sources. Each component keeps its own
license; the application MIT license does not replace third-party licenses.

Client source availability
The release source contains client/package.json and client/yarn.lock with
exact package versions, resolved source URLs and integrity values. Existing
forked modules include mpeg2toh264 (MIT, copyright 2026 otya), mpegts.js,
DPlayer, aribb24.js and web-bml. Their retained notices are in client/.
The browser transcoder's package.json omits a license field, but its included
LICENSE explicitly grants MIT terms; that LICENSE is bundled unchanged.

Runtime tools downloaded separately
The installers fetch pinned official KonomiTV thirdparty artifacts, verify
their SHA256 before extraction, and retain their license files. FFmpeg and
the other runtime programs retain their respective licenses. They are not
embedded or republished in this installer. See INSTALLATION.md for the pinned
build workflow/source commit, download URLs, checksums and expiration behavior.
